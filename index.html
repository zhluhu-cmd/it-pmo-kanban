How this board works

Four habits cover almost everything a PMO analyst does on a Kanban board. Read them, then try them on the Board tab.

Step 1

Read the board

Each column is a status. Each card is one task with its ID, priority, owner and due date. The bar at the top shows the whole portfolio at a glance.

Step 2

Move work forward

Drag a card to another column or to a status tab, or use Move ▸ on the card. Use a status tab to focus on one stage at a time.

Step 3

Spot risk early

An Overdue badge means the due date has passed and the task is not Done. Blocked cards need someone to remove the dependency.

Step 4

Log new work

Choose + Add Task. The card appears at once, and the demo tries to send an email notification in the background.

Priority guide

The coloured edge and label on each card show how urgent it is.

  • CriticalService or security impact now. Escalate today.
  • HighImportant and time-bound. Review daily.
  • MediumPlanned work with normal pace.
  • LowNice to have. Schedule when capacity allows.
